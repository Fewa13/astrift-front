Last updated: November 27
Updates:

Desktop & Mobile Reliability
- Fixed secondary touches taking over movement, aiming or attack controls; switching movement modes now releases held controls.
- Ancient Age now initializes after mode selection, with the previous mode released first.
- Improved cached-resource consistency and offline recovery.
- Prevented delayed cloud saves from overwriting newer settings or affecting a newly selected account.

BUG FIXES — Community Maps
- Removed hidden 48-monster and 16-scripted-monster truncation. Authored targets and valid scripted summons retain their world state; distant idle AI updates less often while nearby and engaged monsters remain responsive.
- Fixed wandering monsters ignoring monster-only walls. Wander movement, path checks and ground height now consistently use the monster collision layer.

Mobile Interface

- The Inventory, Character, Adventure, Social and Shop category buttons can now be moved and resized individually in the screen layout editor, with saved positions.
- Equipped gear is collapsed by default on mobile. Expand it to inspect or change gear; its height is limited to preserve space for inventory items.
- Reduced spacing around the mobile inventory title, categories and auxiliary links. The item area scrolls independently.

Ancient Entry and Map Selection

- Moved previous dungeon cleanup into the loading phase with an explicit progress stage.
- Prepare the official map selector before loading finishes; Enter opens it directly without another cleanup request.
- Dungeon cleanup still applies on entering/leaving the game, while reconnects retain their state.
- Pause background 3D rendering while map selection is open, then resume on exit to reduce menu overhead.

Friend Voice and Mobile Controls

- Fixed scrolling on the mobile profession page; profession and skill content now scrolls with touch.
- Added Start voice chat to Ancient and FPS friend conversations. Friends can accept or decline; the caller sees declined, unanswered, cancelled and ended states.
- Accepted calls provide one-to-one voice with mute, unmute and hangup, using a landscape-friendly mobile panel.
- Release local audio when calls end, are declined, disconnect or microphone setup is cancelled. Only friends and call participants can connect. Call audio is not stored.

Mobile Login Fix

- Login and registration pages now scroll vertically, keeping their bottom actions reachable in landscape.
- Authentication and password recovery adapt to the visible area when the keyboard opens, including embedded pages.
- Ancient login no longer shifts out of view due to vertical centering; recovery dialogs stay above the login page.

Map Loading, Voice Access and Skill-book Choices

- Official maps and trial scenes are built during startup loading, with material compilation and texture preparation before entry. Map changes reuse prepared scenes; loading shows the current map being prepared.
- Voice call buttons now appear beside each friend, with another entry inside the conversation.
- Selectable skill books list only regular professions that actually use books; authority and fusion professions are excluded.
- Action notices such as friend-request confirmations disappear after about 3.5 seconds.

Selectable Skill Books and Trial Exits

- Selectable books from mail, the tower and puzzle rewards now go to Inventory → Consumables when claimed.
- Tap skills to add or remove copies, including across regular professions. Use any selected amount; unspent choices remain. No forced full redemption or typed quantity.
- Selection stops at the owned amount. Invalid, duplicate or excessive requests are rejected without spending items.
- Leaving dungeons or profession trials opens map selection instead of automatically entering the Tribe. Late dungeon updates cannot pull players back into an exited run.

Map Entry Loading

- Startup prepares only the initial scene and map selector rather than warming every map at once.
- Selecting a map opens a dedicated loading bar for map data, materials, textures, graphics preparation and the world connection.
- Graphics warmup uses a one-pixel viewport and waits for GPU completion instead of queueing full-map draws.
- Return to map selection during loading or after a connection failure. Previously built scenes are reused.

First Entry After a Full Restart

- First map selection builds a lightweight menu instead of reinserting menu nodes previously mounted during startup.
- The lobby Enter button opens Official Maps rather than restoring My Maps and waiting for its network request.
- Game pages opt out of third-party live text scanning to reduce extension interference.

Lobby Entry Responsiveness

- The lobby renders its background once, avoiding continuous 3D submissions while entering menus. Normal rendering resumes in the map.
- Map selection removes repeated full-map gradients and hover filters while keeping region shapes, colors, landmarks and entries.
- High-resolution checks use actual pointer input and cover the first entry after a full restart.
